Texas veterans — interactive charts

These slots are for Flourish embeds. Build each chart in Flourish, publish it, then paste the embed snippet into this file (see steps below). Load the Flourish script once at the bottom of this page.

How to add your Flourish chart
  1. In Flourish, click Export & publish → Embed in a website.
  2. Copy the <div class="flourish-embed" …> line for your visual.
  3. In this file, find the matching table section and replace the placeholder <div class="embed-shell">…</div> with your Flourish div.
  4. At the very bottom of this page, keep one line: <script src="https://public.flourish.studio/resources/embed.js"></script>
